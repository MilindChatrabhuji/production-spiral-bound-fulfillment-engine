### 1. ENGAGEMENT SCOPE & MANUFACTURING SPECIFICATIONS
1.1 Service Provider will provide digital-to-print imposition, multi-vendor print-on-demand routing, coil binding calibration, and international carrier tracking for Client's physical book editions.  
1.2 All physical editions shall conform to strict binding standards:
- **Binding Style:** Mechanical Plastic Spiral Coil (4:1 pitch) or Twin-Loop Wire-O (3:1 pitch) facilitating 360-degree flat-lay orientation.
- **Punch Margin Safety Zone:** Minimum **0.50 inches (12.7mm)** inner margin clearance strictly preserved from perforation.
- **Paper Durability:** Acid-free paper meeting ANSI/NISO Z39.48 standards.

---

### 2. REPRINT DEFECT WARRANTY & REMEDIATION
2.1 **Zero-Defect Commitment:** Service Provider warrants that delivered copies shall be free from physical binding defects, page misalignments, missing leaves, and ink smudges.  
2.2 **Defect Remediation:** If a delivered volume displays punch intrusion into text, coil separation, or transit water damage, Service Provider will dispatch a replacement copy at zero additional cost within 7 calendar days of photographic notification.
