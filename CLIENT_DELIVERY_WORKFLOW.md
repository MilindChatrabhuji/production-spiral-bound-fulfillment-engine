# CLIENT DELIVERY & ONBOARDING WORKFLOW: SPIRAL-BOUND BOOK FULFILLMENT

**Workflow Reference:** CDW-POD-SPIRAL-2026  
**Audience:** Account Directors, Publishing Production Managers, Author Clients  

---

## 1. PHASE 1: MANUSCRIPT INTAKE & GUTTER PREFLIGHT (DAY 1 - 3)
1. **Digital Asset Intake:** Ingest single-page PDF manuscript, high-resolution CMYK cover spread, and ISBN metadata.
2. **Automated Gutter Scan:** Execute imposition engine to verify alternating 0.50" (36pt) inner gutter binding margins.
3. **Clinical Content Review:** Ensure all medical manuals and clinical protocols contain peer-reviewed citations and required disclaimers.

---

## 2. PHASE 2: IMPOSITION MANIFEST & DIGITAL PROOF APPROVAL (DAY 4 - 6)
1. **Calculate Coil Gauge:** Automatically determine required mechanical coil diameter (8mm - 32mm) based on sheet caliper.
2. **Preflight Proofing:** Generate 3D digital PDF proof displaying punch perforation lines and flat-lay margins.
3. **Client Sign-Off:** Obtain formal client sign-off on paper stock (80# satin vs synthetic Teslin) and cover poly lamination.

---

## 3. PHASE 3: PHYSICAL GOLDEN PROOF & FLAT-LAY AUDIT (DAY 7 - 10)
1. **Pilot Print Run:** Route single golden proof to Lulu Direct or local regional POD hub.
2. **Mechanical QA Inspection:** Verify 360-degree flat-lay turning, punch alignment, and 45-degree inward coil crimping.
3. **Physical Approval:** Ship golden proof to Client for hands-on review.

---

## 4. PHASE 4: POD API INTEGRATION & CARRIER WEBHOOK BINDING (DAY 11 - 13)
1. **Connect Storefront Webhooks:** Bind Shopify / WooCommerce / custom checkout webhooks to edge fulfillment worker.
2. **Configure Multi-Vendor Routing:** Activate automated geographic routing (Lulu for US/EU, Regional POD Hub for India/Asia).
3. **Carrier Webhook Configuration:** Configure automated tracking dispatch via SMS/WhatsApp upon carrier dispatch.

---

## 5. PHASE 5: COMMERCIAL GO-LIVE & MONTHLY RETAINER ACTIVATION (DAY 14)
1. **Storefront Publication:** Enable physical spiral-bound edition ordering on digital storefront.
2. **Delivery Guarantee Active:** Activate 100% reprint defect warranty and 24/7 carrier tracking portal.
3. **Retainer Onboarding:** Activate monthly managed workbook operations retainer for continuous title management.
